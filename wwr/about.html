White Water Rafting

At White Water Rafting, we specialize in world-class guided excursions through the breathtaking canyons of Moab, Utah. Our expert guides are dedicated to providing safe, high adrenaline adventures that are sure to connect you with the raw beauty of the many rivers we offer. Whether you are seeking a peaceful family cruise or a challenge on our Snake Rapids, we deliver the ultimate white water experience.

History

Founded in 1994 by William Water, our company began as a small family operation with just 2 rafts and a passion for the untamed waters of the Colorado River. What started as a local hobby quickly transformed into a premier adventure as word spread about our guides incredible knowledge of the Moab canyon systems. Over the last 3 decades, we have expanded our fleet and our reach, welcoming tens of thousands of explorers each year.

We survived the record-breaking flood season of the late nineties, which only strengthened our commitment to absolute safety training and high-quality equipment. Our history is built on a foundation of respect for the water and a desire to share the thrill of the great outdoors with people from all walks of life. Today we stand as a leader in the industry, honored by the countless memories we've helped create beneath the towering red rock cliffs.
